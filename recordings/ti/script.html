Tigrinya recording script

46 takes · 327 sounds and words · about 30–45 minutes

How to record

  1. Quiet room with soft things around (a closet full of clothes is great). Fans and AC off, phone in airplane mode.
  2. Use Voice Memos. Hold the phone 15–20 cm from your mouth and keep that distance the whole time.
  3. One recording per take. Read left to right, each sound once, then pause about 2 seconds before the next.
  4. Say each sound clearly, the way you'd teach it, not stretched out. For letters, say just the sound (ለ = “lä”), not the letter's name.
  5. Made a mistake? Stop, delete that recording and redo the whole take. They're short.
  6. Rename each recording to its take number (1, 2, … 46). Order doesn't matter, just the number.
  7. Send all the recordings to the Mac (AirDrop, or Share → Save to Files) and put them in recordings/ti/raw/.
